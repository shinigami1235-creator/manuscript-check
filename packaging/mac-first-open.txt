Installing Manuscript Check on a Mac

1. Drag Manuscript Check onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click Manuscript Check.

The first time, the Mac blocks it since it is not registered with Apple. Click Done, then:

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer
    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the line about
    Manuscript Check and click Open Anyway. Type the Mac password if asked, then click
    Open Anyway again.

  macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older
    In Applications, hold Control and click Manuscript Check, choose Open, then click Open.

After the first time it opens with a double-click and updates itself.

Word files
  Checking Word files needs Microsoft Word or LibreOffice. The first time you check one,
  click OK when the Mac asks to let Manuscript Check control Microsoft Word, and Allow
  if it asks to access data from other apps.
