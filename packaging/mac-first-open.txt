Installing Manuscript Check on a Mac

1. Drag Manuscript Check onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click Manuscript Check.

The first time, the Mac says it cannot check the program for malware, because it is not
registered with Apple. Click Done, then:

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer
    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the line about
    Manuscript Check being blocked and click Open Anyway. Type the Mac password if asked,
    then click Open Anyway again.

  macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older
    In Applications, hold Control and click Manuscript Check, choose Open, then click Open.

This is needed once. After that it opens with a normal double-click and updates itself.

Word files
  Checking a Word file needs Microsoft Word or LibreOffice on the Mac. The first time,
  the Mac asks if Manuscript Check may control Microsoft Word. Click OK. If it also asks
  to access data from other apps, click Allow. Word only opens a copy of the file.
  PDF files need nothing else.
